# Strategic points

Status: idea.

## Behaviour
- Special locations (e.g. a transmitter tower or landmark) that can only be captured by physically reaching the point.
- The first player to reach it owns it.
- The owner upgrades it by visiting again: up to **level 3**, with a **1-day cooldown** between upgrades.
- To take it over, another player must visit it as many times as its level.

Example: someone captures a tower and visits it twice → level 2. Others must visit it twice to take it.
